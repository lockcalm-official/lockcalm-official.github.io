Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

1. Local Storage (Privacy by Design)

All photos, locations, and timestamps recorded by LockCalm ("the App") are stored locally on your device only. We do not transmit or collect your personal data on any external servers.

2. Location Data

The App uses your device's location (GPS) solely to record the physical spot where your check was performed. This data remains on your device and is never uploaded externally.

3. Camera and Photos

The App uses your camera solely to take photos of your lock or status checks. These images are saved locally on your device.

4. Advertising

The free version utilizes Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers according to Google's privacy policy.

5. In-App Purchases

Payments and lifetime license entitlements are managed securely via RevenueCat, Apple App Store, and Google Play. The developer never stores or accesses your credit card or payment details.
